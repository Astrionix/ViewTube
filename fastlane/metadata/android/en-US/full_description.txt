ViewTube is an unofficial YouTube client for Android phones and tablets. It provides a touch interface with liquid glass aesthetics, its own Media3 player and offline saving.

<b>Your account, in seconds</b>
Sign in with a code, the way a TV does: Google's own page opens, you allow access, and you're back. Your subscriptions, history, playlists, Watch later and recommendations load from your account. It doesn't need microG, GmsCore, root or a patched app. Signing in is optional.

<b>Keeps playing</b>
Background playback with the screen off, picture-in-picture, and a mini-player you can drag down while you keep browsing. Lock-screen and headset controls work too.

<b>Save for offline</b>
Pick a quality up to 1080p, or keep only the audio. Saved videos play in the same player with no connection.

<b>Less noise</b>
SponsorBlock skips sponsor segments, DeArrow swaps clickbait titles and thumbnails, and Return YouTube Dislike shows estimated dislike counts. There are no Shorts anywhere in the app.

<b>Works with ViewTube on your TV</b>
Pair with ViewTube on the TV using its Remote control code, play straight to a Chromecast from the phone (no live streams or subtitles in that mode), or use the TV's own YouTube app.

<b>Also</b>
• Up to 4K where the video and the phone allow (1080p by default)
• Subtitles, including auto-translated tracks
• Playback speed from 0.25x to 2x
• Playlists, queue, several accounts
• Read comments and live chat; chapters and dubbed audio tracks
• Picture-in-picture on Android 8 and later
• No analytics, no crash reporting, no ad SDKs. MIT licensed.
