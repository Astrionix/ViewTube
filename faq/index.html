About ViewTube

Is ViewTube official?

No. ViewTube is an independent, unofficial open-source project (MIT licensed). It is not affiliated with, funded, authorized or endorsed by Google LLC or YouTube.

What makes ViewTube special for phones?

ViewTube is built specifically for smartphones: a fluid liquid glass touch interface, gesture-driven volume and brightness, picture-in-picture, background audio playback with screen off, offline downloads, and direct InnerTube client access without Google Play Services or microG.

How is it different from NewPipe, LibreTube or ReVanced?

They're all good apps, and which one fits depends on what you want. NewPipe and LibreTube don't sign in to a Google account; they keep your subscriptions on your phone or on a Piped server. ReVanced patches the official app and needs GmsCore unless you're rooted. ViewTube uses Google's standard Device Code flow, so you access your real account, subscriptions, and history directly without Google Play Services or root.

Is ViewTube open source?

Yes. ViewTube is fully open source under the permissive MIT license. Anyone can inspect the code, build APKs from source, report issues, or contribute improvements.

Was it made with AI?

Yes, in large part. ViewTube is maintained with modern AI coding assistants, Claude and Codex; the commit trailers say so. The maintainer directs and reviews that work and uses the app daily. Each release record lists what was checked on a real phone, what was checked only with automated tests, and what is still open.

Why no donations?

ViewTube is completely free and community-driven. It does not accept donations or sell subscriptions. If you enjoy ViewTube, consider sharing the app with friends.

Install and updates

Which APK do I pick?

arm64-v8a fits almost every phone from the last eight years or so. armeabi-v7a is for older 32-bit phones, universal works on any ARM phone if you're not sure (it's bigger), and x86 is for older x86 devices and emulators. ViewTube needs Android 7.0 or newer. All four are on the download page.

Does it need Google Play Services, microG, root or ReVanced?

No. ViewTube isn't a patched YouTube app. It signs in with a code the way a TV does, so it doesn't need Google Play Services or a stand-in for them, and its APK contains no Google Play Services libraries.

How do updates work?

ViewTube checks its GitHub releases for a new version and offers it in the app; Android's installer then installs it over the old one, keeping your account and settings. You can also add https://github.com/Astrionix/ViewTube to Obtainium, or install a newer APK over the old one yourself.

Is it safe? Who signs the APK?

Every ViewTube APK is signed with the same key, which belongs to the maintainer, @aleixrodriala. Since 1.10.3 the APKs are built and signed by GitHub Actions from the tagged source, and each file has a build attestation. Each release lists a SHA-256 checksum and a VirusTotal scan for every APK. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting and no ad SDKs. How to check each of these is on the trust page.

Will Google's developer verification block ViewTube?

Not for now, according to Google. The requirement that started on 30 September 2026 covers apps installed from participating stores, such as Google Play, in some regions and on certified Android devices. Google's FAQ says that if users sideload an app directly, “these new verification requirements won't apply to your app yet.” ViewTube is installed from GitHub, so it is sideloaded. Google plans a global rollout in 2027, with an “advanced flow”: a one-time setup that lets users install apps from unverified developers after acknowledging the risks. What ViewTube does about 2027 hasn't been decided yet.

Your account

How do I sign in?
  1. Open the You tab and tap the account row. ViewTube shows a short code.
  2. Tap Continue with Google. Google's own page opens: pick your account and allow access. It may mention a TV, because ViewTube signs in the same way a TV does.
  3. Come back. Sign-in finishes by itself.

Your password is only ever typed into Google's page, never into ViewTube, and signing in is optional. The login token is stored on your phone and never sent to the developer (backups you turn on can include it). You can revoke it at any time at myaccount.google.com/security, under Your connections to third-party apps & services.

Can I use more than one account?

Yes. ViewTube keeps several accounts with a switcher, or none at all: once you have one, the account row opens the switcher, where you can add another. Signed out, YouTube still personalises Home from what you watch on that phone.

Using it

Can I save videos for offline?

Yes. Pick a quality up to 1080p, or keep only the audio. Saved videos play in the same player with no connection, and they show up in your gallery. Subtitles aren't saved with them, live and upcoming videos can't be saved, and a playlist can't be saved in one go. Save only what the law and the platform's terms allow you to.

Does it keep playing with the screen off?

Yes. It plays in the background with lock-screen and headset controls, and in picture-in-picture on Android 8 and later. Swipe the video down and it keeps playing in a mini-player while you browse.

Can I watch on my TV?

Yes. You can cast videos to a Chromecast, Android TV, or Smart TV directly from ViewTube, or link using a TV remote code.

Are there Shorts?

No. Shorts don't appear in Home, Subscriptions, History, search, channels, playlists or Up next, and autoplay skips them. A Shorts link someone shares still opens, in the normal player.

Which languages does it speak?

ViewTube's interface is in English and Spanish, and core messages support about 45 languages. Help with more is welcome: see TRANSLATING.md.

Problems

A video won't play. What now?

YouTube sometimes refuses a video or an account. ViewTube retries through other routes, but some videos still won't play: some only play in YouTube's own app or after a purchase, such as paid movies and some music. If one keeps failing, open an issue with the video link and your ViewTube version.
